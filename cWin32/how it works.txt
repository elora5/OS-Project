How the implementation works (brief)

API: GetCursorPos(&POINT) from User32 gives you global screen coordinates of the cursor in pixels.

Loop: A simple while loop polls the cursor, prints it with \r to rewrite the same line, and sleeps N ms with Sleep(N) to control the refresh rate and CPU usage.

Resilience:

SetConsoleCtrlHandler lets us exit cleanly on Ctrl+C or window close.

SetConsoleMode removes ENABLE_QUICK_EDIT_MODE and ENABLE_MOUSE_INPUT so the console never suspends the process when clicked/selected.
